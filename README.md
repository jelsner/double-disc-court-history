# Double Disc Court History

This site hosts a readable historical essay on the evolution of Double Disc Court (DDC), focusing on rules, organization, and play culture.

The historical source material is drawn primarily from the archives and publication material of the Flying Disc Museum:
https://www.flyingdiscmuseum.com/

The current version was prepared with AI assistance and human editing.

Live site:
